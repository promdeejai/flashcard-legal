⚠️ Not legal advice. This policy was written to accurately describe how the App currently works, kept in sync with the code as features change. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer — consider having one review it before wide public release.

This policy explains what the App does with information. The guiding principle: your study data stays on your device and there are no accounts. A few optional features send small amounts of data off your device — to third-party services, to a database we use to improve word suggestions, or (if you turn it on) to a crash-reporting service to help us fix bugs — described below.

1. Summary (the important parts)

  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no behavioral analytics, no tracking, no ad ID. The one exception is an optional, OFF-by-default crash reporter (§2.5) — it never tracks what you do in the App, only technical details at the moment of a crash.
  • Your decks, cards, study history, settings, and captured words are stored only on your device (a local SQLite database and local app storage).
  • Some optional features send limited data off your device:
    • Translation/definitions → individual words/short phrases sent to MyMemory and Free Dictionary API.
    • Vocabulary suggestions (optional, OFF by default) → if you turn this on in Settings, the word, definition, and your "My Language" setting of cards you save are sent to our database, anonymously — with no account, device, or user identifier attached. Never your example sentences, images, or any other card content.
    • Google Drive backup (optional, OFF by default) → if you connect your Google Drive, a backup of your own data is saved to a folder in your own Drive, using a Drive access scope limited to files the App itself creates.
    • Crash reporting (optional, OFF by default) → if you turn this on in Settings, technical crash/error details (stack trace, device model, OS and app version) are sent to our crash-reporting service. Never your flashcards, account, or anything that identifies you.
  • You can turn off every off-device feature in Settings, and delete all data by clearing app data or uninstalling.

2. Information the App processes

2.1 Stored only on your device (never sent to us)

  • Decks, cards (words, definitions, examples, optional images you add), and study/review history.
  • App settings (theme, language, learning settings, notification settings).
  • "Captured words" inbox (text you captured via text-selection, pending review).
  • Your consent record (timestamp + policy version), stored locally as evidence of your choices.

2.2 Text you capture from other apps (optional)

If you use the system "share to / process text" feature to send selected text to the App, that text is stored locally in your captured-words list.

2.3 Vocabulary suggestions (optional, OFF by default)

If you turn on Help Improve Suggestions in Settings, the word, definition, and your "My Language" setting (e.g. "Thai" — the language you chose for translations, not your device's language or location) of each card you save from then on is sent to a database we use to build better vocabulary suggestions, so we can match suggestions to other learners using the same language. This is:

  • Anonymous — no account, device ID, or any other identifier is attached to the row. We cannot trace a submitted word/definition/language back to you or your device.
  • Minimal — only the word, definition, and language code. Never your example sentences, images, deck names, or study history.
  • Opt-in and reversible — OFF unless you turn it on, and you can turn it back off at any time in Settings; doing so stops all future sharing (previously shared, anonymous entries cannot be identified as yours to delete individually, precisely because nothing ties them to you).

2.4 Google Drive backup (optional, OFF by default)

If you connect your Google account in Settings → Backup & Restore, the App can save a backup of your own decks, cards, study history, and settings into a folder ("Flash Card Data") in your own Google Drive, and restore from it later. This uses Google's drive.file scope, which limits the App to files it created itself — it cannot see or access the rest of your Drive. This data is controlled by you in your own Google account; we never receive a copy of it.

2.5 Crash reporting (optional, OFF by default)

If you turn on Help Fix Bugs in Settings, the App starts sending crash and error reports to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so we can find and fix bugs. This is:

  • Technical only — a crash report contains the error's stack trace, your device model, OS version, and the App's version. Never your flashcards, account, or any other content you've entered.
  • Not behavioral tracking — it does not record what you tap, how you use the App, or build any profile of you. It only activates at the moment something goes wrong.
  • Off by default, and nothing is sent until you turn it on — the crash reporter is not started at all while this is off, not merely "started but filtered," so no crash data — not even at the device level — is captured while disabled.
  • Reversible — turn it off in Settings at any time to stop all future reports.

3. Device permissions and why

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to show capture suggestions and optional study/quiz reminders.
  • Foreground service: used by the quiz-notification scheduler.

4. Why we are allowed to process this (legal bases)

Where the Thailand PDPA, GDPR, or similar law applies, our bases are:

  • Your consent, which you give in the App's onboarding and by enabling optional features, and which you can withdraw at any time by disabling those features in Settings; and
  • Performance of the service you requested and our legitimate interest in providing the core flashcard functionality you asked for.

5. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are largely in direct control:

  • Access / portability: view your data in the App and export decks to CSV.
  • Erasure: delete decks/cards in the App, clear the App's data, or uninstall to remove everything from your device.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off vocabulary sharing, Drive backup, crash reporting, or any other optional feature in Settings, at any time.

Where the law gives you additional rights (objection, restriction, complaint), contact us at suvijak.in@gmail.com. If you are in Thailand you may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC); in the EU/UK, to your local supervisory authority.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will help you delete the on-device data.

7. International transfers

The optional third-party/cloud services in Section 2.6 may process data on servers outside Thailand (e.g., in the United States or the EU). By using those optional features you understand the relevant data leaves your device to those providers under their own safeguards.

8. Security

Data on your device is protected by your device's own security (lock screen, OS sandboxing). We cannot protect data after it leaves your device to a service you chose to use (translation/definition lookups, vocabulary sharing, or your own Google Drive). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Retention

On-device data is kept until you delete it (in-app, by clearing data, or by uninstalling). Anonymous vocabulary-suggestion entries (§2.3), once submitted, contain no identifier and so cannot be linked back to you to delete individually; we retain them to keep improving suggestions. Drive backups (§2.4) live in your own Google Drive under your control. Crash reports (§2.5), if you opt in, are retained by Sentry per their own retention policy (currently 30 days) and are not otherwise linked to you.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. If changes are material, we will update the effective date and ask for renewed consent in the App where required. The current version always ships with the App.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: suvijak.in@gmail.com.
